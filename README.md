Objective:
Analyze credit and debit transaction data to identify trends, patterns, and insights that can inform business decisions, improve customer targeting, and enhance risk management.

Tool used:
1. MS Excel
2. My SQL
3. Power BI
4. Tableau

Business Need:
1.Understand customer behavior
2.Improve customer targeting
3.Enhance risk management

Insights and Recommendations:
1. Customer spending patterns: Identified trends in transaction habits and spending behavior.
2. High-risk customer segments: Identified customer segments with high credit risk.
3. Targeted marketing opportunities: Developed targeted marketing campaigns for high-value customer segments.

Conclusion:
1. Improve customer understanding: Gained insights into customer behavior and transaction habits.
2. Enhance risk management: Identified potential credit risk and developed mitigation strategies.
3. Drive business growth: Developed targeted marketing campaigns and product offerings to drive business growth.
By leveraging data analytics, the bank can make informed decisions, improve customer targeting, and enhance risk management.
